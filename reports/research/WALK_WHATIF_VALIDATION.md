Generated by `scripts/validate_walk_whatif.py` from `experiments/twin_validity/protocol_addendum_v4.json`. Synthetic simulator only; a research estimator; not clinical evidence and not exercise advice.

Walk: +125 steps/min for 20 min (2,500 steps) starting 15 min after the meal. Truth: paired simulator difference (meal + walk minus meal only) in noiseless interstitial glucose.

The served tree models see only past steps (30 and 60 min before the forecast moment), so they cannot represent a planned walk; the twin has no learned per-patient activity parameter, so every candidate is cohort level.
